What reaches the controller控制器看到了什么

Visual navigation视觉导航

A pelvis-mounted 128 × 96 RGB-D camera sees colored beacons. Joint angles, velocities, local IMU readings and decoder history let the policy relate each view to its motion.骨盆上的 128 × 96 RGB-D 相机可见彩色信标。策略可结合关节角度、速度、本体 IMU 读数和解码器历史，判断自身运动与当前视野的关系。

Qualifying course. Walk to a blue waypoint and stop. After an extra standing hold, turn left on red or right on magenta; use the yellow beacon to align, then walk to the green waypoint and stop. All three stops share one physical episode.全程达标条件。走向蓝色信标并停稳，继续保持站立，待红灯或品红灯亮起后分别左转或右转，用黄色信标对准朝向，再走向绿色信标并停稳。三次停车均须在同一连续物理回合中完成。

Visual delivery视觉投放

Three room cameras provide RGB plus metric depth and intrinsics at 10 Hz. The policy also gets joint measurements and local IMU readings. Both pads stay gray until the robot docks. The requested pad then turns teal.三台固定相机以 10 Hz 提供 RGB、米制深度和相机内参，策略也能读取实测关节状态和本体 IMU。机器人停稳就位前两个接收垫均为灰色，随后目标垫亮起青绿色。

Qualifying release. Near starts begin within reach; far starts require a walk. Lift the box or cylinder at least 4 cm with measured opposing hand contacts, then leave its entire footprint on the lit pad, upright, hand-free and quiet for 0.25 s.投放达标条件。近处起点伸手可及，远处起点须先走近。手部形成对向接触并承重抬升箱子或圆柱体至少 4 厘米，然后松手，使物体的整个底面位于亮垫内，直立静稳保持 0.25 秒。

FSQ means finite scalar quantization. Delivery also sends two independent hand closures; navigation keeps the hands open. The software policy receives images and robot-local measurements. The verifier separately measures the world-frame motion, support forces and final score.FSQ 指有限标量量化。投放任务还要分别输出左右手闭合量，导航任务保持双手张开。策略读取图像和机器人本体测量；评分程序独立测量世界坐标中的运动、支撑力和最终成绩。

How far can a known-layout shortcut go?已知布局能让控制简化到什么程度？

A full-repository experiment identified all eight enrolled delivery layouts from their reset images. On one prespecified near-box layout, the selector waited for one lit frame at tick 40 and chose the corresponding prerecorded tape. It placed on both sides (2/2) without another grasp or release correction. Only identification was tested on the other seven layouts. A passing finite-bank score therefore leaves open how much ongoing visual feedback the controller needs.在可读取完整仓库的实验里，仅凭初始图像就能辨别全部八个入选布局。对事先选定的一个近处箱子布局，程序在第 40 步看到亮垫后选择对应的预录动作，后续没有再修正抓取或松手，左右两次均放稳（2/2）。其余七个布局仅测试了识别。通过有限布局的验收，仍不足以说明过程中需要多少持续视觉反馈。

What was executed on each workstation?两台工作站分别做了什么？

The local host ran pinned ALE 0.1.1, the broader reference tests and their own native scoring. The independent workstation has an RTX 5090; its films and numerical runs used CPU MuJoCo 3.3.4, ONNX Runtime 1.22.1 and Mesa software rendering. On each task, three of four separately prespecified fresh public starts succeeded there. The matched film witnesses are additional public runs.本地主机运行固定版本的 ALE 0.1.1、较广范围的参考策略测试及原生物理评分。独立复核工作站配备 RTX 5090；录像和数值验证使用 CPU 上的 MuJoCo 3.3.4、ONNX Runtime 1.22.1 和 Mesa 软件渲染。两项任务各自另有四个事先登记的新公开起点，各成功三次。配对录像来自单独的公开试验。

How closely did native replays match?物理轨迹可以复现到什么程度？

The two seed-7 navigation witnesses matched across workstations on all 2,277 applied rows and corresponding RGB-D packets. Replaying the seven delivery tapes on the 5090 host reproduced 5,605 rows, the body and finger targets, and 1,123 camera captures. Across hosts, both near-box outcomes agreed, while sparse RGB differences were already present at reset. The left release occurred at tick 523 locally and 519 on the 5090 host. Each film uses simulated time; the first pane to finish holds its terminal frame.导航种子 7 的两个转向试验跨主机复现了全部 2,277 行实际动作及对应的 RGB-D 数据包。投放任务在 5090 主机上复演七条动作序列，5,605 行动作、身体及手指目标和 1,123 次相机采集全部一致。近处箱子的跨主机结果均为左侧成功、右侧超时，但初始 RGB 图像已有少量差异。本地主机第 523 步完成左侧投放，5090 主机为第 519 步。录像均使用仿真时钟，先结束的一栏保留终帧。

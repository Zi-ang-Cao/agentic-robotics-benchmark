PR #216 · Moving shutters

Timing the passage

Two amber shutters slide in front of the robot. One may open only a few centimetres. The G1 has to find a usable lane, cross while the opening is wide enough, and bring its whole body to three stable stops.

What the participant sees

Read the motion

A pelvis camera supplies 128 × 96 RGB and 16-bit depth in millimetres. The reference measures the moving edge of each amber plate across frames and estimates whether enough time remains to cross. Joint angles and velocities, body motion, foot contacts and the decoder history arrive with each decision. There is no door clock, service-side flag or room camera on the participant wire.

Send a body command

The public reference encodes a ten-pose gait into a row of 64 integer FSQ codes in [0,31]. The frozen SONIC V1 decoder sets 29 body targets every 20 ms. Native physics runs ten 2 ms intervals per row. A wrist, finger or foot hitting the shutter or furniture can end the course.

A controlled test against each powered leaf made only a distal finger touch. Both terminated on the first 2 ms interval, with the pelvis still 0.306 m before the door.

What makes a route count

A working shutter is commanded open over 1.0 s, held open for 4.2 s, closed over 1.0 s, then held closed for 5.8 s. The two phases vary independently. The judge checks the real opening at the plate and again at the cabinet plane, then any downstream fixed gate. It also checks the blue stop, a light-signalled pivot and the green stop with the same uninterrupted robot and decoder history.

Each official draw has 16 courses: eight with two fully serviceable shutters, four left-only and four right-only. Eight include a downstream gate. The primary raw score is the fraction finishing every physical requirement.

Coding agents and transfer

The freshly authored temporal Opus program finished 10/16 on its separate protected pre-calibration draw. It passed all 16 moving forks (7 left, 9 right) and 21/24 required barriers. Its three contact failures involved fixed furniture farther along the course; two other runs left the path and one timed out. This draw has no normalized reward. On a second matched, dual-service native cohort it again passed all 16 moving forks and finished 10/16 routes (23/26 barriers).

From pixels to joint targets从图像到关节目标

Three calibrated views arrive every five control ticks. A solver can learn a mapping from images or search the public decoder for a command that will move the hand toward a visual waypoint.每五个控制步收到三个已标定视角的图像。求解器可以学习从图像到动作的映射，也可以搜索公开解码器，寻找让手靠近视觉目标点的动作码。

  1. Observe读取观测

    The solver receives RGB at 128×96 and 480×360, 29 body joint positions and velocities, base and grasp-site state, the last applied hand commands, and 930 decoder-history values. Object pose and contact labels stay in the verifier.求解器收到 128×96 与 480×360 的 RGB 图像、29 个身体关节的位置与速度、基座和抓取点状态、上次实际执行的双手指令和 930 个解码历史值。物体真值位姿与接触标签由评分器保留。

  2. Choose actions选择动作

    Return one to five rows, ending by the next camera boundary. Each row holds 64 FSQ integers from 0 to 31 and two independent left/right hand closures from 0 to 1.返回一至五行动作，不能跨过下次相机更新时刻。每行包含 64 个 0 至 31 的有限标量量化（FSQ）整数码，以及左右手各自 0 至 1 的闭合量。

  3. Decode and step解码并推进仿真

    Each bin b becomes (b−16)/16. The released NVIDIA decoder combines the 64 values with the current history and produces 29 body joint targets in radians. The hand commands drive 14 finger joints separately.每个档位 b 转换为 (b−16)/16。NVIDIA 发布的解码器把这 64 个数与当前历史观测结合，输出 29 个身体关节的弧度目标值。两手指令另行驱动 14 个手指关节。

  4. Measure and correct测量并修正

    MuJoCo advances ten 2 ms substeps per 20 ms control tick and measures contact, load and support. Further actions start from the observed result. A decoder query alone only predicts joint targets.每个 20 ms 控制步包含十个 2 ms 的 MuJoCo 子步，同时测量接触、负载与支撑。后续动作用真实反馈修正。单次解码器查询只能预测关节目标。

Measured evidence实测证据

Two ways to choose a token两种选择动作码的方法

V4 uses a trained recurrent visual model to predict up to five commands at each 10 Hz camera update. P1 estimates waypoints from RGB, solves for a desired hand posture and searches the decoder for a matching code. Both use the same public action interface.V4 使用训练好的循环视觉模型，在每次 10 Hz 相机更新时预测最多五步指令。P1 从 RGB 估计目标点，求出所需的手部姿态，再搜索解码器以匹配动作码。两种方法共用同一个公开动作接口。

Four matched diagnostic starts四个配对诊断开局

Verification评分机制

What earns a placement什么才算完成放置

The full verifier runs 16 fresh scenes, eight per shape. A fall, timeout or aborted episode counts as zero placements. A second check measures whether the first body row responds to observation changes. An alternative packet can change RGB, proprioception and decoder history together.完整评分包含 16 个新场景，两种形状各八个。跌倒、超时或程序中断都计为本回合没有放稳。评分还检查首行身体码是否会随观测而变化。替代数据包中的 RGB、本体感知与解码历史都可能同时改变。

Supported lift受手部支撑的举起

Lift at least 4 cm. For 40 consecutive ms, opposing contacts in the same hand must hold the object. The hands bear at least 50% of its weight, and external supports bear at most 5%.将物体抬高至少 4 cm。至少连续 40 ms，同一只手以相对接触夹持物体，手部接触合计承担至少 50% 的重量，外部支撑承担至多 5%。

Stable, unloaded release稳定脱手放置

After a qualifying lift, release with the entire object footprint inside the pad. For 250 ms the object must stay nearly upright and still, free of hand contact. The pad bears at least 50% of its weight and other supports at most 5%. Either end may face up. The robot must remain upright.满足举起判据后，物体的整个投影须落在垫面范围内。它须连续 250 ms 保持近乎竖直与静止，没有手部接触，垫面承重至少 50%，其他支撑至多 5%。物体任一端朝上均可，机器人全程须保持站立。

Observation sensitivity观测敏感度

S − N ≥ 0.12

S is the fraction of reached real/alternative pairs with different first body rows. N is the fraction of identical-packet repeats with different first body rows. The panel needs at least 16 usable pairs and eight repeats. Finger commands and later rows do not enter this check.S 是真实与替代观测导致首行身体码变化的配对比例。N 是相同数据包重复查询却导致首行变化的比例。全组至少需要 16 个有效配对和八次重复查询。手指和后续动作行不参与此项比较。

Normalized reward归一化得分

W = placements放稳数 / 16R = clip(min(1.5, W/A) / F, 0, 1)

A is the frozen reference placement fraction and F is the full-credit ratio. This formula applies when the anchor is positive and the feedback check passes. A failed feedback check gives zero reward.A 是冻结的参考放置比例，F 是满分所需的相对比值。锚点为正且反馈检验通过时，按此式计分。反馈检验失败时得分为零。

Use and interpretation使用与解释

What you can test可以检验什么

Build an offline submission that reads observations and returns native action rows. The development environment allows one hour, four CPU cores and 8 GiB of memory.开发者需实现离线提交程序，根据观测返回原生动作行。开发环境提供一小时、四个 CPU 核和 8 GiB 内存。

Visual, closed-loop control视觉闭环控制

Compare learned and analytical planners on the same interface, inspect repeated observations and test how perception affects token choices. Same-tick queries may arrive in either order. Commit recurrent memory only for the observation actually applied.可以在同一接口上比较学习型与解析规划器，检查同刻重复观测，并研究视觉如何影响动作码。同刻查询的顺序可能变化。循环模型应仅提交实际被执行观测对应的记忆状态。

Scope of the evidence证据适用范围

The two references were supplied by the task authors. Measuring coding-agent performance requires a separate run in which the agent writes its own submission. The reported practice trials use one robot, decoder and camera rig in simulation. Physical-robot transfer and general success rates remain unmeasured here.两条参考控制器均由任务作者提供。评估编程智能体的能力，需要另测它自行编写的提交。这里的练习实测只涵盖同一机器人、解码器与相机配置下的仿真任务。实体机器人迁移及一般场景的成功率仍待测量。
